These settings are saved on this device only. They will not follow you to another phone or computer, and clearing your browser data for this site will remove them. There is no account to sign in to.
Install this app
It's a website, so there is nothing to install just to look at it — but you can add it to your home screen and it will open full-screen, with no browser bar, like an app. No app store, no account.
iPhone & iPad
On iPhone and iPad this only works in Safari. Chrome, Firefox and Edge on iOS cannot add an app to the home screen — if you are reading this in one of those, open the tournament link in Safari first.
  1. Open the tournament link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The icon appears on your home screen.
Android
  1. Open the tournament link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen — or Install app, if Chrome offers that directly.
  4. Confirm. The icon appears on your home screen.
Installing is optional. Everything works in a normal browser tab — the installed app just opens full-screen and keeps working on a weak connection at the field.
